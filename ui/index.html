One claim, five stages.
Nobody chases anybody.

  1. 1.0 Request

    Pick a template, fill a short form.

    Travelling, food or a hotel stay. The form is drawn from the template, and you see what happens next before you submit.

  2. 2.0 Approve

    Managers decide one after another.

    Each approver is told only when it is their turn. The amount picks who has to approve:

    • L1 up to ₹25,000, Reporting Manager
    • L2 up to ₹75,000, + Head of Department
    • L3 up to ₹2,00,000, + Head of Division
    • L4 above that or abroad, + MD
  3. 3.0 Advance

    Money before you travel, if you asked.

    Once every manager has approved, Finance releases the advance and the claim moves on by itself.

    60%

    of the estimated cost, at most. Ask for more and the form tells you straight away.

  4. 4.0 Settle

    Photograph each bill. The amount is read for you.

    A hotel invoice claimed as a cab ride is flagged and left out. The same bill twice is caught. You never type an amount.

  5. 5.0 Paid

    Finance verifies, the Controller pays.

    You are told when the money is dispatched, or how much comes out of payroll if your bills came in under the advance.
